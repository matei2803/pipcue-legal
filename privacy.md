## 2. Our Privacy Approach

PipCue is designed around a local-first model.

The content of your conversations is processed on your Android device. PipCue does not upload your notification content, sender names, conversation names, or message bodies to developer-controlled servers.

PipCue does not require a PipCue account.

PipCue does not sell personal data.

PipCue does not contain advertising.

## 3. Notification Access

PipCue uses Android Notification Access to identify conversations that may require a reply.

Android grants notification-listener applications broad access to notifications displayed on the device. PipCue uses this access only for the functionality described in this policy.

PipCue filters notifications and processes eligible notifications from communication applications that PipCue supports and that you choose to monitor, such as WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Gmail, Slack, and Telegram.

Depending on the notification provided by the source application, PipCue may locally access information such as:

- the application that generated the notification;
- sender or conversation name;
- notification title;
- notification or message text;
- conversation identifiers or notification metadata;
- timestamps;
- whether a notification belongs to an individual conversation, group, summary, or other notification type;
- other structured Android notification information required to identify, group, and manage follow-ups.

PipCue does not use Notification Access to create a remote history of unrelated notifications.

Notification Access can be disabled at any time through Android system settings. If it is disabled, PipCue will no longer be able to automatically detect new conversations requiring follow-up.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

PipCue may store the following information locally on your device:

- pending conversations;
- sender or conversation labels;
- relevant notification or message content;
- timestamps;
- monitored application selections;
- snooze status and snooze times;
- ignore rules;
- conversation state;
- application preferences;
- appearance and theme settings;
- reminder preferences;
- aggregate local usage statistics relating to completed follow-ups.

This information is used to provide PipCue's core functionality.

Sensitive conversation information is kept within PipCue's local application storage and is excluded from Android backup where supported by the application's backup configuration.

## 5. How We Use Information

Information processed by PipCue is used to:

- identify conversations that may require a reply;
- merge notifications belonging to the same conversation;
- display pending follow-ups;
- prioritize unresolved conversations;
- provide snooze, done, ignore, and reminder functionality;
- open the relevant source application when requested;
- maintain local application preferences;
- provide local statistics;
- determine access to PipCue Pro features.

We do not use the content of your notifications for advertising.

We do not use message content to build advertising profiles.

## 6. Information Sent Off Your Device

PipCue does not transmit your notification content, message bodies, sender names, or conversation names to developer-controlled servers.

PipCue also does not send this conversation content to advertising services, analytics services, or billing providers.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Conversation information remains locally on your device while PipCue needs it to provide the functionality you requested.

When you mark a conversation as **Done**, PipCue removes the stored conversation after updating applicable local aggregate statistics.

When you **Ignore** a conversation, PipCue removes the current conversation and may retain a local ignore rule so similar notifications can continue to be ignored.

Snoozed conversations remain locally stored until they become active again, are completed, ignored, or otherwise removed.

Application preferences and local statistics may remain until you clear PipCue's application data or uninstall the application.

Because PipCue does not maintain a cloud account containing your conversation history, we generally cannot remotely access or delete conversation data stored only on your device.

You can remove PipCue's locally stored data by:

1. using available deletion or completion controls within PipCue;
2. clearing PipCue's storage through Android system settings; or
3. uninstalling PipCue.

Information associated with Google Play purchases is controlled separately by Google and may be retained according to Google's policies and applicable law.

## 10. Security

We use technical and architectural measures intended to limit unnecessary exposure of personal information.

These measures include:

- local processing of conversation content;
- Android application sandboxing;
- avoiding transmission of conversation content to developer servers;
- excluding sensitive PipCue data from Android backup where configured;
- limiting use of Notification Access to PipCue's stated functionality.

No software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You should keep your Android device protected with appropriate device security controls.
